Pick a dictionary extension if

you want a quick lookup of a word you half know, and you are happy to skim the meanings. They are good at that, and several of them are excellent.

Pick Gloss if

you are reading something dense — a newspaper, a paper, a book — and the words you do not know are the ones whose meaning depends on the sentence. That is the case a dictionary popup cannot help with, because it cannot see the sentence.
